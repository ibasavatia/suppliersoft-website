Procure-To-Pay

Automate the end-to-end procurement cycle — from requisition and purchase order through receipt and invoice reconciliation — with supplier collaboration and ERP integration.

Business Challenge Key Features Business Benefits
Business Challenge

Manual procurement means slow cycles and costly errors

Procure-to-pay spans requisitions, approvals, purchase orders, receipts, invoices, and payments — often handled through a mix of email, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems. The result is slow cycle times, limited spend visibility, and errors in matching invoices to orders.

Companies need a digitized, controlled procure-to-pay process that connects buyers and suppliers and flows cleanly into their back-end ERP.

Key Features

End-to-end procurement, from requisition to payment

Digitize and control the full P2P cycle, with suppliers collaborating directly on the platform.

  • Requisitions and approvalsRaise requisitions and route them through configurable approval workflows, enforcing procurement policy and controls.
  • Purchase order managementCreate and manage purchase orders, with clear status and purchase-order summary reporting.
  • Supplier collaborationSuppliers confirm orders and provide information directly, reducing back-and-forth email.
  • Receipt and invoice reconciliationMatch receipts and invoices to purchase orders, with invoice reconciliation reporting to catch discrepancies.
  • Spend visibilityStandard reports — purchase-order summaries, invoice reconciliation, and budget-versus-actual — give visibility into spend.
  • ERP integrationIntegrate with back-end ERP systems such as SAP and Oracle for seamless data flow.
  • Secure, auditable processingRole-based access and secure data handling support controlled, auditable procurement.
