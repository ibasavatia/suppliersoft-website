The platform

One supplier portal, built on Salesforce

SupplierSoft solutions are built and hosted on the Salesforce Platform, one of the world's leading enterprise cloud platforms. Through our OEM partnership with Salesforce, we deliver enterprise-grade functionality, scalability, and security. All your team and your suppliers need is a web browser and an internet connection.

Use a single supplier portal

Manage any supplier-facing process on one platform

In addition to the standard solutions, the SupplierSoft supplier portal can be configured to automate any supplier-facing business process. Customers typically start with one high-ROI process, knowing they can add others over time — all through one supplier login and one consolidated view of each supplier.

  • Built on the Salesforce PlatformRuns on Salesforce's multi-tenant, cloud SaaS platform, giving you enterprise security, reliability, and global performance without managing infrastructure.
  • Nothing to installA cloud-native subscription service — no software to install or maintain, with secure access from anywhere via a web browser.
  • High supplier adoption by designSuppliers respond through familiar formats and no-login survey links, so there are no registrations, training, or custom web forms standing in the way.
  • One portal for every processCompliance, quality, risk, qualification, and procurement all share one supplier portal and one supplier record.
  • Configurable without ITPoint-and-click configuration adapts workflows, forms, dashboards, and reports as regulations, customers, and your supply base change.
  • 360-degree supplier visibilitySee supplier interactions across purchasing, quality, compliance, engineering, manufacturing, and accounts payable in one place.
  • Extends your ERPComplements and integrates with back-end ERP systems such as SAP and Oracle, managing the supplier processes ERPs don't.
