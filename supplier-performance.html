Supplier Performance & Scorecards

Turn data scattered across ERP systems, internal teams, and suppliers into consistent, configurable scorecards — with automatic roll-ups and closed-loop follow-up on issues.

Business Challenge Key Features Business Benefits
Business Challenge

Most supplier scorecards are static spreadsheets

Supplier performance data lives in different places: on-time delivery in the ERP, quality data in another system, and subjective ratings in people's heads. Pulled together manually in spreadsheets, scorecards become backward-looking reports that are hard to keep current and hard to compare across suppliers.

Without a consistent, automated way to define, gather, and roll up performance data — and to act on what it shows — performance management stalls and issues go unaddressed.

Key Features

Configurable scorecards, fed automatically, acted on in one place

Define what performance means for your business, then let the system gather and roll up the data.

  • Back-end integrationData that resides in back-end systems, such as on-time delivery, can be integrated directly into scorecard management.
  • Automatic roll-upsThe solution rolls scores up based on your configurable scorecard definition, providing both absolute and percentage scores.
  • Internal surveysInternal users can be surveyed to provide supplier ratings for various measures, which roll up into the supplier's score for that measure.
  • Supplier self-serviceSuppliers can review their current and past performance through self-service, driving transparency and improvement.
  • Integrated CAPAClosed-loop corrective and preventive action ensures performance issues that need action are tracked and monitored to closure.
  • Scorecard dashboards and reportsConfigurable dashboards and reports provide visibility into supplier performance across your supply base.
