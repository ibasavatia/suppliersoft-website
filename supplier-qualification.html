Onboarding

Supplier Qualification & Information

Onboard and qualify new suppliers through configurable approval workflows, and maintain a single, trusted source of supplier information that every department can rely on.

Business Challenge Key Features Business Benefits
Business Challenge

Supplier onboarding and information is fragmented across departments

Qualifying a new supplier touches purchasing, quality, compliance, engineering, and finance — each collecting its own information in its own way. The result is duplicated requests to suppliers, inconsistent data, and slow onboarding that delays getting products to market.

Once a supplier is onboarded, their information — contacts, certifications, banking, capabilities — ages and scatters across systems, so no one has a single, current, trustworthy view of each supplier.

Key Features

One place to qualify suppliers and keep their information current

Structured onboarding plus a central information repository gives every team the same trusted supplier data.

  • Configurable qualification workflowsDefine multi-step and parallel approval processes so the right functions review and approve each supplier, consistently.
  • Supplier self-entered informationSuppliers provide their own information through survey links without logging in, reducing training and keeping data first-hand.
  • Central supplier information repositoryMaintain a single source of supplier master data — contacts, certifications, capabilities, and documents — shared across departments.
  • Document managementStore and search supplier documents and certifications centrally, with expiration tracking so nothing lapses unnoticed.
  • Reminders and refreshAutomatically request updates so supplier information stays current over time.
  • 360-degree supplier viewQualification and information sit alongside risk, quality, and compliance data on the same platform, for one consolidated view of each supplier.
