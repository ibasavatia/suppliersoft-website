Business Challenge

Growing outsourcing means growing supplier risk

As outsourcing increases, manufacturers depend on their suppliers more than ever for customer success. That has reduced cost, but at the price of greater supplier risk and less visibility into supplier operations, finances and location-based exposure.

Risk data is typically scattered across spreadsheets, emails and individual buyers' knowledge, making it hard to see concentrations, compare suppliers consistently, or respond quickly when conditions change. Manual risk reviews and delayed onboarding let issues slip through the cracks.

To manage risk proactively, companies need a single, consistent way to define risk, gather it from suppliers, and quantify it across the supply base.

Key Features

Define risk your way, then quantify it across every supplier

The solution turns scattered risk information into consistent, comparable metrics.

  • Configurable risk profileConfigure the risk factors that matter — financial information, geo-political location, and business-criticality factors such as direct versus indirect procurement or the product lines involved.
  • Risk-assessment surveys with no supplier loginMass-email a configurable risk-assessment survey with a link. Suppliers click and respond without logging into the system, reducing the need for supplier training.
  • Automated response trackingThe system automatically tracks which suppliers have responded, with reminders, and provides comprehensive scoring and reporting on the collected data.
  • Configurable risk metricsRoll components of the risk profile up into one or more configurable quantitative supplier-risk metrics, which you can use to prioritize risk-mitigation strategies.
  • Prioritized mitigationRank and segment suppliers by risk so your team focuses effort where exposure is highest.
  • One consolidated supplier viewRisk sits alongside quality, compliance and performance data on the same platform, for a 360° view of each supplier.
