Business Challenge

Supplier contracts get lost in inboxes and shared drives

Supplier contracts are often scattered across email, shared drives, and filing cabinets, with approvals chased manually and renewals tracked in spreadsheets. Contracts expire unnoticed, obligations go unmet, and it is hard to find the current version when it matters.

Companies need a single, secure place to create, approve, store, and renew supplier contracts — with visibility into status and upcoming renewals.

Key Features

The full contract lifecycle in one system

Manage supplier contracts from creation through approval, signature, storage, and renewal.

  • Centralized contract repositoryStore all supplier contracts in one secure, searchable location, so the current version is always easy to find.
  • Creation and approval workflowsCreate contracts and route them through configurable multi-step approval workflows across the right functions.
  • Electronic signaturesExecute contracts with electronic signatures, removing print-sign-scan delays.
  • Renewal and expiration alertsAutomatic alerts flag upcoming renewals and expirations so contracts don't lapse.
  • Status visibilityTrack where every contract is in its lifecycle, from draft to signed to renewal.
  • One consolidated supplier viewContracts sit alongside qualification, performance, and risk data on the same platform.
