Supplier Surveys

Business Challenge Key Features Business Benefits
Business Challenge

Collecting data from suppliers is slow, manual, and hard to track

Almost every supplier process — risk, compliance, qualification, performance — depends on getting accurate information back from suppliers. Done through email and spreadsheets, that means chasing who received the request, who responded, and who is past due, then re-keying answers by hand.

Traditional web-form tools make it worse by forcing suppliers to register, learn a portal, and log in — friction that drives response rates down. Companies need a way to survey suppliers that is effortless for the supplier and automatically organized for the buyer.

Key Features

One survey engine behind every process

The same survey capability powers risk assessments, compliance declarations, qualification, and performance data collection.

  • Configurable surveysBuild surveys for any process — risk, compliance, qualification, performance — with the questions and logic you need.
  • Mass email with a click-through linkSend a survey to your whole supplier base by email. Suppliers click the link and respond directly, with no login required — reducing training and driving higher response rates.
  • Automated trackingThe system automatically tracks who received the survey, who responded, and which responses are past due.
  • Expiration-driven remindersAutomatic reminders ensure suppliers respond before the due date, without manual follow-up.
  • Scoring and roll-upResponses are scored and can be rolled up into quantitative metrics — for example, into supplier risk or performance scores.
  • Dashboards and reportsConfigurable dashboards and reports give you visibility into responses and results across the supplier base.
  • Reusable across the platformBecause the survey engine is shared, data collected once can feed multiple processes on the same platform.
